## Account

## Catalog

**[CO₂ rating](/catalog/terms/co2-rating.md)**:
A grade from A to E shown on each product; the catalog can be sorted by it and filtered to eco-friendly products.

**[Product](/catalog/entities/product.md)**:
A tool for sale, with name, price, category, brand, CO2 rating, description, specifications and stock.

## Ordering

**[Cart](/ordering/entities/cart.md)**:
