# Areas

* [Account](account/) - Customers sign in, sign out and manage their account
* [Catalog](catalog/) - Shoppers browse, search, sort and filter tools and view product details
* [Ordering](ordering/) - Shoppers collect products in a cart and check out to place an order
* [Support](support/) - Visitors contact the shop with questions

# Key entities

* [Product](catalog/entities/product.md) - A tool for sale, with name, price, category, brand, CO2 rating, description, specifications and stock. _(unverified)_
* [Cart](ordering/entities/cart.md) - The products a shopper has chosen, with quantity, line totals and a cart total. _(unverified)_
* [Order](ordering/entities/order.md) - A placed purchase with a billing address, payment method and an invoice number. _(unverified)_
